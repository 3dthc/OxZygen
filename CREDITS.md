# OxZygen: sample sources, credits and licenses

OxZygen only ships sounds that may be redistributed and used commercially. Every accepted source is CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication): no royalties and no attribution required. Credits are kept anyway, for transparency.

## In use

| Source | Author | License | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) | Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner | CC0 1.0 (LICENSE in repo) | Flute (sustain, vibrato); French horn, muted trumpet, tenor trombone, clarinet, oboe; violin, viola and cello sections (sustain and tremolo) |
| [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) (fetched from the mirror [trbngr/VCSL](https://github.com/trbngr/VCSL)) | Versilian Studios LLC | CC0 1.0 (README) | Ocarina (typical and small); tubular bells, vibraphone, bowed vibraphone, wine glasses |
| [Karoryfer Weresax](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.weresax) | Karoryfer Samples | CC0 1.0 (LICENSE in repo) | Alto saxophone |
| [SpectMorph instruments](https://github.com/swesterfeld/spectmorph-instruments) (vowel recordings only) | Stefan Westerfeld and contributors | CC0 for the claudia, mirko and sven vowel sets | Choir vowels (ah, ih, oh) |

Synthesized formant, bell, synth (Pad, Lead, Brass) and texture layers are generated by the plugin and use no recorded material.

## Fonts
- [Barlow Condensed](https://github.com/jpt/barlow) by the Barlow Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (`vst/skin/fonts/OFL.txt`). Baked into the skin artwork for labels and titles. MPC draws live values in its own
  Titillium Web.

## Not used (not redistributable in a plugin)
- Vienna Symphonic Library, including its free instruments and choirs: its license forbids redistribution and derivative sample products.
- Other SpectMorph instruments: separate license; only the CC0 vowel sets above are used.
- Royalty-free loop and sample packs (Splice, Looperman and similar): cleared for music production, not for redistribution inside a plugin.
- Sonixinema Brute Flute, FluidR3 GM pan flute: redistribution terms not confirmed.
