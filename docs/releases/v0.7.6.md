# OxZygen v0.7.6

## What's new
- **Seven new recorded instruments**, in two new sound categories on the SOUND page:
  - **Keys:** grand piano, electric piano, concert harp.
  - **Mallets:** glockenspiel, hand chimes, kalimba, marimba.
  - They work like the bells: Decay, Shimmer and Tone knobs, and they layer with any other sound. The download grows from 131 MB to 169 MB.
- **Tempo sync:** two pickers on the FX page.
  - **Delay SYNC:** FREE (the Time knob, as before) or a note value: 1/16, 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8D, 1/4T, 1/4, 1/4D, 1/2, at your project's tempo.
  - **MOTION:** FREE (as before) or one sway per 4 bars, 2 bars, 1 bar, 1/2 bar or 1/4 note. While the song plays it's locked to the bar. Vibrato stays free.
  - Works on the MPC, in MPC Desktop and in Logic. With no tempo from the host, it uses 120 BPM.
- **104 new presets (200 in total):**
  - **Up-front sounds** meant to be the main sound: synth leads, plucks, poly keys, analog brass, stabs, basses, string machine, solo flute, sax, clarinet and trumpet, vocal leads and choir hits, evolving and pulsing pads.
  - **KEYS** (17, new): the new instruments on their own (Grand Piano, Soft Piano, Electric Piano, Concert Harp, Kalimba, Marimba, Glockenspiel, Hand Chimes) and paired with pads, strings and bells.
  - **NOIR** (20, new): sad, somber sounds: a worn electric piano, a felt-soft piano, leads that cry and waver, pads that sound like they're behind a wall, lonely vocal hooks.
  - **ASTRO** (16, new): dark and spacey: breathy flutes, far-off bells and mallets, whining leads, distant horn signals, deep space pads and choirs.
  - **GEMS** (20, new): beautiful, sparkling sounds, each built on a different instrument: soft hand chimes over a low bed, music-box glockenspiel, kalimba and glass, harp and glass, glittering pads, and four reed-and-synth leads voiced to be played high.
- **BROWSE page:** 12 categories in two columns, with larger text for the categories, the preset names and the headings. The Sync pickers' text on FX is larger too.

## Fixed
- **Notes ringing on after Stop:** the host's Stop now lets go of every note and the sub layer, and a note whose release never arrived is let go once no key is down. Release, reverb and echo tails still ring out.
- The 96 existing presets sound the same as before and keep their places in the preset list, so saved projects open as they were.
