# OxZygen v0.7.6

## What's new
- **Tempo sync:** two pickers on the FX page.
  - **Delay SYNC:** FREE (the Time knob, as before) or a note value: 1/16, 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8D, 1/4T, 1/4, 1/4D, 1/2, at your project's tempo.
  - **MOTION:** FREE (as before) or one sway per 4 bars, 2 bars, 1 bar, 1/2 bar or 1/4 note. While the song plays it's locked to the bar, so it lands the same on every pass. Vibrato stays free.
  - Works on the MPC, in MPC Desktop and in Logic. With no tempo from the host, it uses 120 BPM.
- **72 new presets (168 in total):**
  - **36 up-front sounds** meant to be the main sound, not just a layer: big chorused synth leads, plucks, poly keys, analog brass, stabs, basses, string machine, bell and glass keys, solo flute, sax, clarinet and trumpet, vocal leads and choir hits, and a few evolving and pulsing pads. They're spread across the existing categories (most in SYN), and several use the new sync.
  - **NOIR** (20, a new category): sad, somber sounds: warm, blurry keys and soft plucks, leads that cry and waver, pads that sound like they're behind a wall, lonely vocal hooks.
  - **ASTRO** (16, a new category): dark and spacey: breathy flutes, far-off bells and mallets, whining leads, distant horn signals, deep space pads and choirs.
  - All built from OxZygen's own sounds, so the download is the same size.
- **BROWSE page:** the category list now holds 10 rows (All plus 9 categories).

## Fixed
- The 96 existing presets sound the same as before (their delay and Motion stay FREE), and saved projects open as they were.
