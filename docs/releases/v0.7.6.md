# OxZygen v0.7.6

## What's new
- **36 new presets in two new categories:**
  - **NOIR** (20): sad, somber sounds that carry a melody on their own: warm, blurry keys and soft plucks, leads that cry and waver, pads that sound like they're behind a wall, lonely vocal hooks, and a slow funeral bell-and-trombone.
  - **ASTRO** (16): dark and spacey: breathy flutes in the fog, far-off bells and twinkling mallets, whining alien leads and a slow siren, distant horn signals, and deep space pads and choirs.
  - Built from OxZygen's own sounds, so the download is the same size. They're on the BROWSE page under NOIR and ASTRO, and in MPC's PRESET menu.
- **BROWSE page:** the category list now holds 10 rows (All plus 9 categories).

## Fixed
- Nothing else changes: the 96 existing presets sound the same, and saved projects open as before.
