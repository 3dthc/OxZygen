# OxZygen v0.7.7

## What's new
- **Instruments browser card:** OxZygen now shows as its own card in Sounds > INSTRUMENTS. Tapping it opens the plugin's preset list (after the restart the installer does).
- **Presets on the Instruments card:** tapping the OxZygen card in Sounds > INSTRUMENTS now lists all 200 factory presets; pick one to load OxZygen with that sound.
- **Updated zips (2026-11-17):** if you downloaded this release earlier today, download it again and reinstall: the presets on the Instruments card are now grouped in their category folders, and preset names with a hyphen no longer split into their own folders. Projects are not affected.
