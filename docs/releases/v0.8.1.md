# OxZygen v0.8.1

## What's new
- **20 new presets for melodies and chords**, ethereal and sparkling but made to play: they speak the moment you hit a pad and hold steady pitch, so chords stay sweet and fast lines come through. They sit in your existing banks:
  - **VOX:** Seraph Lead, Choirboy Melody, Hymn Chords
  - **WND:** Crystal Flute, Fairy Ocarina, Moonlit Clarinet
  - **BEL:** Starfall Vibes, Bell Choir Melody, Singing Glass
  - **KEY:** Celestial Harp, Stardust Piano, Dream Kalimba
  - **GEMS:** Prism Lead, Wishing Well, Sapphire Keys
  - **ASTRO:** Nebula Flute, Satellite Bells
  - **NOIR:** Last Slow Dance, Rainy Window Keys
  - **SYN:** Halo Saw Lead
- **16 near-duplicates replaced with new sounds**, in the same banks and list positions:
  - Glass Piano → Moonlight Celesta
  - Piano and Pad → Piano Halo
  - Chord Keys → Glow Keys
  - Midnight Marimba → Glass Marimba
  - Solo Flute → Silver Flute Lead
  - Solo Trumpet → Gilded Horn Lead
  - Neon Stab → Crystal Poly
  - Opal Pluck → Topaz Harp
  - Moonstone → Moonstone Choir
  - Velvet Bells → Velvet Lantern
  - Smoke Ring Sax → Velvet Sax Melody
  - Static Pulse → Faded Music Box
  - Echoes Of You → Echo Of A Kiss
  - Astronaut Fog → Astronaut Lullaby
  - Abduction → Starship Choir
  - Violet Planet → Violet Comet
- **Q-Links on the Browse page:**
  - **Q-Link 1** steps through the categories (All, Voices, Winds, Brass, Strings, Bells, Synth, Air, NOIR, ASTRO, GEMS, Keys).
  - **Q-Link 2** steps through the presets in the category you're on and loads each one as it lands, so you hear them as you scroll.
  - The data wheel works the same way.

## Fixed
- **Leads that swelled in or drifted:** 43 lead presets (flutes, saxes, vocal leads, synth leads) now start right away and wobble less. Among the biggest changes: Lean Flute, Lone Flute and 4AM Hook Vox now speak in about 20–30 ms instead of 0.2–0.4 s, and Clay Ocarina and Far Planet Pipe hold pitch about twice as steady.
- Pads and beds are unchanged.
- **Browse Q-Links were not mapped,** so turning them moved unrelated controls. Now every click moves exactly one category or one preset, even on a fast turn, and both stop at the ends of the list instead of jumping back to the start.

220 presets in total. Saved projects keep their sounds, and their preset numbers still point to the same presets.
