# OxZygen v0.8.2

## What's new
- **Q-Links on the Browse page:**
  - **Q-Link 1** steps through the categories (All, Voices, Winds, Brass, Strings, Bells, Synth, Air, NOIR, ASTRO, GEMS, Keys).
  - **Q-Link 2** steps through the presets in the category you're on and loads each one as it lands, so you hear them as you scroll.
  - The data wheel works the same way.

## Fixed
- **Browse Q-Links were not mapped,** so turning them moved unrelated controls. Now every click moves exactly one category or one preset, even on a fast turn, and both stop at the ends of the list instead of jumping back to the start.

Saved projects and presets are unaffected.
