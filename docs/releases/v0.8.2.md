# OxZygen v0.8.2

## What's new
- **New card on the MPC Sounds screen:** the OXZYGEN tile now matches the current look, with the chamber's lit vent slats behind a brushed-metal nameplate, replacing the old orb picture.
- **Browsing with Q-Links and the data wheel, like the Odin 2 port:** on the Browse page, the first Q-Link knob picks the bank (ALL and each of the 11 banks) and the second picks a preset. On ALL, the preset knob steps through every preset in order. The data wheel steps through them too.

## Fixed
- **Browse page Q-Links:**
  - The preset knob moved several presets per click; it now takes a few clicks per preset, so it's easy to stop on the one you want.
  - At the end of a bank it got stuck on a "Null" value with numbers; it now stops on the bank's last preset and turns straight back.
  - The page showed more than one Q-Link group. Now there's one group: bank and preset.
  - The other Q-Link knobs could move Sound page controls (A Spread, A Tone) on the Browse page. They now do nothing there.
  - The bank knob skipped ALL. It now reaches ALL, and on ALL the preset knob stays on ALL instead of jumping to another bank.

Sound, presets and saved projects are unchanged.
