# OxZygen v0.8.2

## What's new
- **New card on the MPC Sounds screen:** the OXZYGEN tile now matches the current look, with the chamber's lit vent slats behind a brushed-metal nameplate, replacing the old orb picture.
- **Browsing with Q-Links and the data wheel, like the Odin 2 port:** on the Browse page, the first Q-Link knob picks the bank and the second picks a preset in that bank. The data wheel steps through them too.

## Fixed
- **Browse page Q-Links:**
  - The preset knob moved several presets per click; it now takes a few clicks per preset, so it's easy to stop on the one you want.
  - At the end of a bank it got stuck on a "Null" value with numbers; it now stops on the bank's last preset and turns straight back.
  - The page showed more than one Q-Link group, and other knobs could reach other controls. Now there's one group: bank and preset, with every other slot empty.

Sound, presets and saved projects are unchanged.
